# The Undying Flame

A cult — and, older than the cult, a being. Its mark is a white skeletal hand
clenching a flame that burns up through the palm, painted in
[the Undercroft](../locations/the-undercroft.md) beneath the University's
off-campus dorm. The four students who vanished from that dorm were not taken.
They were its cell, hoarding fire components and dissecting suture flies in the
dark under their professors' feet.

## The Myth

Older than [Utsa Paradisa](../locations/utsa-paradisa.md) itself. Two very
powerful beings once contested for the love of an elven woman living in the
desert. One of them won her, and from that union came a daughter — half genie,
half elf, blue-skinned and translucent. The being who lost has never been named
in any tale [Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md) could recall from the academy; its true
name was never known. It is remembered only as **the Undying Flame**.

The daughter is [Naivara Tidewoven](../npcs/naivara-tidewoven.md). The winner,
by his own account, is the fey being sealed inside her teardrop amulet —
[the Amulet Speaker](../npcs/the-amulet-speaker.md), who took one look at the
sigil and said he had thought it might be him: *"this old so-and-so, never could
let it go. He's always been jealous of me and my lover."*

If that is right, the abduction of the water priestess is not a raid on the
city's water supply. It is the last move in a grudge older than the city.

## Known Cell

- **Cassian** — evocation.
- **[Celine](../npcs/celine.md)** — divination; warded the dorm against every
  scrying eye that might have caught them.
- **[Varrek Ashtongue](../npcs/varrek-ashtongue.md)** — illusion; left the glyph
  of warding that nearly killed the party on their way out.
- **Calus** — conjuration.

One master of each school taught at [the University](../locations/the-university.md),
and not one of them a necromancer — the only school the city forbids.
