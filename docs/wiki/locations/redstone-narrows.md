# The Redstone Narrows

A canyon country of red rock and tar pits out in the desert beyond
[Utsa Paradisa](utsa-paradisa.md), worked for generations by slavers who camped
there in tents like a war band — hobgoblins and ogres standing over the barrels —
with no fixed hall or stronghold anyone ever saw. It is where
[Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md) and his family were made to harvest tar as
labourers, and where the recon unit that [Behnsun Payne](../pcs/behnsun-payne.md)
rode with was sent undercover, on the Empress's own quiet orders, to free the
slaves. It is also the one place the vanished students' coded notes name.

## Why It Matters

Tar burns slow and hot, and the Narrows sit on more of it than anywhere within
reach of the city. For a cult that calls itself
[the Undying Flame](../factions/the-undying-flame.md) — hoarding guano, fire
components, and everything else that catches — a canyon full of fuel is not a
coincidence. It is also where two of the party's own histories quietly intersect,
long before either of them met.
