# The Undercroft

A hidden warren of stone tunnels and vaulted chambers beneath the off-campus
dorm — a "wizard frat house" on the edge of
[the University](the-university.md) grounds — reached by a trapdoor concealed
under a student's desk. Cold crypt-like passages lit by guttering candle sconces
run off toward the women's dormitories; the rooms below hold a study of
coded notes, chests of raw spell components, barrels of bat guano, a table of
dissected suture flies, and a painted sigil of a skeletal hand clenching a flame.
Whoever worked down here was doing it out of sight of every professor above.

## Notable Features

- **The wooden guardian.** A robed mannequin of solid wood, staff in hand, that
  waited motionless until someone announced themselves — then attacked.
- **The flame sigil.** A crude painting of a white skeletal hand holding a flame
  that burns up through its palm: the mark of
  [the Undying Flame](../factions/the-undying-flame.md). It carries no magical
  residue — it is an idol, not a spell.
- **The circle on the floor.** A ring of small stationary lights the party walked
  all over and could make nothing of.
- **The stores.** Jars of bat guano (a *fireball* component) and chests of plain
  spell components — a hoard tilted heavily toward fire magic.
- **The suture fly table.** Corpses and notes on how to bind the creatures with a
  psychic link to an alpha, which then commands the rest of the swarm.
