# The University

The **University of Sand** — a district of [Utsa Paradisa](utsa-paradisa.md) home
to the city's wizards, shaped like a great hourglass rising and cascading from the
desert, its windows twisting up its sides in a helix. Every school of magic is
taught here **except necromancy**. During the citywide assault it was the district
that fared best, held by "a lot of strong wizards" — though at brutal cost: some
nine or ten students and around fifteen guards fell defending it.

## Who You'll Find Here

[Krum](../pcs/krum.md)'s uncle, the divination professor
[Holgren](../npcs/holgren.md), teaches here, and [Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md) has
connections among its scholars — soon to be a student himself on the Empress's
scholarship. Its arcane affairs are overseen by [Raasha Vaad](../npcs/raasha-vaad.md)
and the war mage [Seerus](../npcs/seerus.md).

## The Missing Students

Four gifted students who shared an off-campus dorm vanished in the chaos of the
attack — neither among the dead nor found alive:

- **Cassian** — human, evocation
- **Celine** — half-elf, divination (a talented loner; Holgren's student)
- **Varrek Ashtongue** — brass dragonborn, illusion
- **Calus** — tiefling, conjuration

Their room showed no struggle and no detectable magic — because Celine had planted
**anti-divination devices** to mask everything within, and left animated guardians
behind.
