# The University

The **University of Sand** — a district of [Utsa Paradisa](utsa-paradisa.md) home
to the city's wizards, shaped like a great hourglass rising and cascading from the
desert, its windows twisting up its sides in a helix. Every school of magic is
taught here **except necromancy**. During the citywide assault it was the district
that fared best, held by "a lot of strong wizards" — though at brutal cost: some
nine or ten students and around fifteen guards fell defending it.

## Who You'll Find Here

[Krum](../pcs/krum.md)'s uncle, the divination professor
[Holgren](../npcs/holgren.md), teaches here, and [Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md) has
connections among its scholars — soon to be a student himself on the Empress's
scholarship. Its arcane affairs are overseen by [Raasha Vaad](../npcs/raasha-vaad.md)
and the war mage [Seerus](../npcs/seerus.md).

## The Missing Students

Four gifted students who shared an off-campus dorm vanished in the chaos of the
attack — neither among the dead nor found alive:

- **Cassian** — human, evocation
- **Celine** — half-elf, divination (a talented loner; Holgren's student)
- **Varrek Ashtongue** — brass dragonborn, illusion
- **Calus** — tiefling, conjuration

Their room showed no struggle and no detectable magic — because
[Celine](../npcs/celine.md) had planted **anti-divination devices** to mask
everything within, and left animated guardians behind. Beneath the dorm's floor —
a trapdoor under a desk — lies [the Undercroft](the-undercroft.md), where the four
of them ran a cell of [the Undying Flame](../factions/the-undying-flame.md) under
their professors' feet.

## The Library

A grand public library with tall double doors, a raised reading area up a short
flight of steps, and more books than [Behnsun](../pcs/behnsun-payne.md) was
willing to risk a *fire bolt* near. It is kept by the elderly librarian
[Bartleby](../npcs/bartleby.md). Student clubs are not its business — those go
through the Administration Building — and its lending records are not opened
without a writ from a captain of the guard.

## Session History

- **[The Tidewoven Amulet](../../sessions/2026-07-03.md)** — Reported intact by
  Seris's aerial scouting; shouts of victory rose from the Wizard Tower as the
  city was secured for the night.
- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — The party came here to
  investigate the four missing students. They found Holgren battered among the dead,
  then entered the students' sealed dorm — where the diviner's animated guardians (a
  mimic, a swarm of flying books, and a smothering rug) nearly killed them all.
- **[The Undying Four](../../sessions/2026-09-02.md)** — The party slept in the dead
  students' beds, opened the hatch beneath the wreckage, and stripped the Undercroft.
  They left through a glyph of warding laid on the door while they slept. The campus
  above was still on its feet but hollow-eyed, with funeral services for the fallen
  students and guards a few hours off.
- **[Varrek's Very Bad Day](../../sessions/2026-10-13.md)** — The party staked out the
  library ahead of the two o'clock funeral services, roughed up its librarian,
  and fought and captured the invisible
  [Varrek Ashtongue](../npcs/varrek-ashtongue.md) among the stacks before chasing
  him out into the city streets. A student put the previous day's dead at twelve.
