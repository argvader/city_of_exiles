# The Water Temple

The temple in [the Jade District](the-jade-district.md) of
[Utsa Paradisa](utsa-paradisa.md) — also called the **Veil of Tides** — where the
water priestess [Naivara Tidewoven](../npcs/naivara-tidewoven.md) performs the rites
that cleanse the city's water and keep it flowing. In a city where water is sacred
and scarce, it is among the most vital sites in Utsa Paradisa. Its greatest rite is
the **Festival of the Four Currents**, when Naivara's labor lets the city hand out
extra water rations — which made the timing of the attack, on the eve of the
festival, look chillingly deliberate.

## Session History

- **[The Tidewoven Amulet](../../sessions/2026-07-03.md)** — The session's climactic
  battle. The party arrived — famously, by catapulting one another across the
  battlefield — to find suture flies stitching Naivara's mouth and hands shut. The
  guard commander [Dalen Marr](../npcs/dalen-marr.md) held the line, but the
  flies carried the priestess out of the city. Before she vanished she dropped a
  teardrop amulet to the party — the session's central clue.
- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — The party began the
  night outside its doors, and learned from the Empress just how much the city
  depends on Naivara: without her sanctifying the water, the dwindling supply grows
  perilous, and the festival hangs in the balance.
