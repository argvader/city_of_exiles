# Bartleby

**Role:** Librarian at [the University](../locations/the-university.md)

## Overview

The old librarian of the University of Sand — very old; old enough that
[Prance](../pcs/prance-galavant.md) wondered aloud whether he predated the city.
Hard of hearing, easily scandalised, and given to "Oh my." On the morning of the
funerals he was minding a nearly empty, short-staffed library, with most of the
University already gone to mourn.

He knows [Krum](../pcs/krum.md) by sight and knows his uncle
[Holgren](holgren.md) well enough to threaten to tell him. He did not recognise
[Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md) as a student.

## Personality

Fussy, proper, and thoroughly out of his depth. He directs enquiries about student
clubs to the Administration Building and will not open the lending records
without a writ from a captain of the guard. He is also a spellcaster of some kind:
fleeing the fight, he touched a hand to his ear and sent a message that brought
two city guards to the doors.

## Relationships

- **[The party](../../sessions/2026-10-13.md)** — Wronged by them. He was accused
  of leading a death cult and put in an armlock before anyone asked his name.
  Prance apologised afterwards, explaining that they were a special task force
  that had saved the city "a little twice."
- **[The City Guard](../factions/city-guard-of-utsa-paradisa.md)** — He called
  them, and told them at length what the five strangers had done in his library.

## Session History

- **[Varrek's Very Bad Day](../../sessions/2026-10-13.md)** — Questioned about
  cults, arrested by Krum on Prance's say-so, and made to survive a Constitution
  save against dying of fright. Released just as an invisible dragonborn started
  bleeding in his reading room, he ran — *"I'm just a librarian!"* — and summoned
  the guards whose arrival let [Varrek](varrek-ashtongue.md) escape.
