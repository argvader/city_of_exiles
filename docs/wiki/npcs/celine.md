# Celine

**Role:** Divination student at [the University](../locations/the-university.md)
· **Status:** Missing

## Overview

A half-elf diviner, one of the four students who vanished from the off-campus
dorm — and the one whose handiwork nearly killed the party twice. The
anti-divination devices that hid the dorm from every searching eye were hers, and
so were the animated guardians left waiting inside it.

## What Holgren Remembers

Her professor [Holgren](holgren.md) taught her and liked her: a hard worker, nose
to the grindstone, comfortably in the top fifteen percent of her class, but a
loner who kept apart from the others. In her last few months her grades slipped.
She had wanted a place on the festival committee — the one that plans the
fireworks show for the Festival of the Four Currents — and was not chosen. It
disheartened her. He never saw her fall in with a different crowd, but then, as
he said, professors do not fraternize.

A talented girl passed over for the fire show, some months before a fire cult's
cell went to ground under her own dorm.
