# Empress Serenity

**Role:** Empress of [Utsa Paradisa](../locations/the-jade-district.md) · **Seat:** the Jade Pyramid

## Overview

The ruler of the City of Exiles, who holds court in the Jade Pyramid flanked by
her Goliath bodyguard [Horace Steelwinter](horace-steelwinter.md). She is
imperious and unabashedly vain — "the most important person in this city, and I
do not say that lightly, because I am *very, very* important" — quick to humble a
loud newcomer ("simmer down, young harengon; speak when you're spoken to") and
quicker to end squabbling at her war table. Beneath the hauteur she is a shrewd
diplomat: she is the one who brokered the fragile peace between the city and the
dragonkin of Drathomir, and she reads the attack on her city as a calculated
strike, timed to the [Festival of the Four Currents](../locations/the-water-temple.md).

Her one irreplaceable asset is the abducted water priestess
[Naivara Tidewoven](naivara-tidewoven.md), whose sanctification keeps the city's
scarce water drinkable — which is why the priestess's loss frightens her more
than she will admit, and why she formally charges the party with getting her back.
