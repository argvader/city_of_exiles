# Frederick

**Role:** Talking fey amulet carried by [Fizz](../pcs/fizz.md)

## Overview

Frederick is a talking wooden amulet — a fey being bound into an idol — that
[Fizz](../pcs/fizz.md) carries and converses with. Only Fizz can hear him, which
makes for some one-sided conversations with a piece of wood. Frederick is
crotchety and comic, quick to withhold his trust, and speaks fondly (and
protectively) of someone he calls "mom."

## The Giraffe

Frederick claims that in a past life he was a **giraffe** — long-necked, eating
leaves off the tallest branches — until "mother" made him a mouse. Fizz's insight
says he believes it absolutely, and the thought of a great majestic beast shrunk
down into something so small has done more to turn Fizz against mother than
anything else he has heard.

## Relationships

- Recognized the spirit in [Naivara Tidewoven](naivara-tidewoven.md)'s teardrop —
  [the Amulet Speaker](the-amulet-speaker.md) — as a "brother," and confirmed
  that Fizz, like [Prance](../pcs/prance-galavant.md), has been "chosen" by a fey
  vessel.

## Session History

- **[The Tidewoven Amulet](../../sessions/2026-07-03.md)** — Vouched (grudgingly) for
  the new amulet and its quest, tying Fizz into the same fey mystery now driving
  Prance.
- **[The Undying Four](../../sessions/2026-09-02.md)** — Held out over the cult's
  coded notes and its sigil, he could offer only that he is a mouse and cannot
  read well, and that none of it reminded him of mother. Then, quietly, he pushed:
  *"Don't let Prance take control. Why does it have to be his tale of glory? Why
  can't it be yours?"* — and, snubbed over the party's name, flopped onto his side
  and sulked.
- **[Varrek's Very Bad Day](../../sessions/2026-10-13.md)** — Kept up the campaign. After
  [Fizz](../pcs/fizz.md)'s spell put the fleeing cultist to sleep, Frederick asked
  again why the rabbit gets to be the hero of every tale — *"It should be you. I
  feel like you saved the day"* — and slumped back onto his side in Fizz's pocket.
  Fizz told him their time to shine would come.
