# Gorvak Skybinder

**Role:** Orc keeper of the Griffin hatchery, envoy of the Iron Holds

## Overview

The gruff orc who manages the city's Griffin hatchery — a gift to Utsa Paradisa
from King Dumaville of the **Iron Holds**, with Gorvak stationed here to watch over
it. An abrasive outsider, tolerated rather than trusted: he needled
[Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md) about his old job mucking out the Griffin stables,
and jabbed that "maybe your jade god isn't as pure as you thought." The Empress
makes no secret of her suspicion of him, reasoning aloud that the Iron Holds would
not gift Griffins for free — and that if they had struck at the city, it would be
to cripple it into dependence.
