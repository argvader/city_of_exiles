# Holgren

**Role:** Divination professor at [the University](../locations/the-university.md); [Krum](../pcs/krum.md)'s uncle

## Overview

An old dwarf who teaches divination at the University of Sand, and
[Krum](../pcs/krum.md)'s uncle — perpetually messy-bearded (blood, ketchup,
mustard; no one can ever tell). He fought through the assault on the University
and came out of it battered and near collapse, having lost some nine or ten
students and around fifteen guards. Of the four missing students, only the
diviner **Celine** was his; he remembered her as a gifted loner but, worn to the
bone, could recall little more.

He handed Krum a **bronze mirror backed with a Gorgon's face** — a keepsake Krum
had been given but "wasn't ready" for before. Now, looking into it, Krum glimpses
a spectral second self behind his reflection, an echo of his Phantom nature.

## A Good Time, Not a Healer

Asked for healing potions by a party that had just been blasted across the campus
lawn, Holgren produced instead two bottles of harsh cinnamon whiskey — *"What am
I, an alchemist, boy?"* — and offered, helpfully, to tell them whether today would
be a good day or a bad one. He cannot cast *cure wounds*. He is a wizard, and a
diviner at that.

## Session History

- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — Met the party amid the
  University's dead, gave what little he knew of the missing diviner Celine, and
  passed Krum the Gorgon-backed bronze mirror. He winced off Prance's "old fey
  remedy" of salt-in-the-wound before Krum could stop it.
- **[The Undying Four](../../sessions/2026-09-02.md)** — Found the party sprawled
  on the campus grounds and fussed over them, poured whiskey into two of them, and
  shared what more he knew of [Celine](celine.md). He asked what they had found
  and was fobbed off; he let it go, said they should talk later, hugged his nephew
  and kissed his forehead — *"you're never too old for that, boy"* — and went to
  help plan the funerals. [Fizz](../pcs/fizz.md) read him closely and found nothing
  but genuine concern. [Prance](../pcs/prance-galavant.md) could get no read on him
  at all, and has decided that means he could be the smartest man in the world or
  valiantly playing the fool.
