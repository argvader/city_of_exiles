# Horace Steelwinter

**Role:** Goliath bodyguard to [Empress Serenity](empress-serenity.md)

## Overview

A mountain of a Goliath — seven feet and change — who stands at the Empress's
shoulder in the Jade Pyramid and rarely lets his stone face crack. He keeps her
war room in order the direct way: when [Salvatore Danilo](salvatore-danilo.md)
propped his boots on the council table, Horace swept them off with the flat of
his enormous hammer and a low "you will not disrespect our war room." Loyal and
protective, he waves off the party as "these idiots" even as he promises the
Empress they'll find Naivara. For all his stoicism, he proved a shockingly
graceful dancer.

## Session History

- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — Kept order through
