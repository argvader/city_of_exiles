# Karza Sunhorn

**Role:** Minotaur leader of the [City Guard of Utsa Paradisa](../factions/city-guard-of-utsa-paradisa.md)

## Overview

The minotaur who commands the city guard — the officer [Dalen Marr](dalen-marr.md)
reports to, and thus [Krum](../pcs/krum.md)'s ultimate superior. Reluctant to
thin the city's defenses by sending guards chasing after the missing priestess,
she struck a compromise: the party could pursue the investigation so long as one
of her own went with them. On Dalen's word that Krum was worthy, she assigned him
as their escort — and, once the tension broke into dancing, asked her subordinate
for a turn on the floor.

## Session History

- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — Authorized the party's
