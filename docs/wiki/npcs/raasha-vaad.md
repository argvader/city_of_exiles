# Raasha Vaad

**Role:** Hadozi administrator of the arcane at [the University](../locations/the-university.md)

## Overview

A Hadozi ("monkey person," as the party less-than-delicately put it) who oversees
arcane affairs at the University of Sand alongside the war mage
[Seris](seris.md). She and Seris held back a grave concern at the council out of
fear for the school's reputation, until pressed: four promising students, all
sharing an off-campus dorm, had vanished in the chaos of the attack — neither
counted among the dead nor found alive. Their rooms held no trace of a struggle
and no detectable magic. Exhausted from the battle at the University, she escorted
the party to the dorm and then, genuinely spent, handed over the key and went to
rest.

## Session History

- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — Broke the news of the
  four missing students to the council, then led the party to their sealed dorm.
  Her "no detectable magic" turned out to be the students' own anti-divination
  devices at work — the room was anything but empty.
