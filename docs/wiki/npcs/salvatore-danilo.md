# Salvatore Danilo

**Role:** Proprietor of the Lucky Scorpion, council member

## Overview

A silk-tongued man of leisure who runs the Lucky Scorpion — the city's casino and
pleasure house — and keeps his own private guard. He is the only figure in the
Empress's war room unbothered by the citywide catastrophe: feet on the council
table, a chalice of wine in hand, needling the grim Goliath
[Horace Steelwinter](horace-steelwinter.md) to come "relax with the girls." He is
acquainted with [Prance](../pcs/prance-galavant.md), and vouched (loosely) that the
party had performed in the wealthier districts.
