# Seerus

**Role:** Amethyst dragonborn professor at [the University](../locations/the-university.md); the Empress's council

## Overview

An amethyst dragonborn professor at the University of Sand, and one of the two
voices the University sends to [Empress Serenity](empress-serenity.md)'s council —
the other being the arcane administrator [Raasha Vaad](raasha-vaad.md), with whom
she sits paired at the table.

She is a soldier before she is a scholar. A former commander turned war mage,
she was [Behnsun Payne](../pcs/behnsun-payne.md)'s superior in the field, and it
was her company that freed [Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md) and brought both of them
to the city four years ago — which is why she can vouch for the pair of them in a
war room full of people inclined to dismiss them.

## Session History

- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — Sat the Empress's
  council beside [Raasha Vaad](raasha-vaad.md), vouching for Payne and Adarius out
  of their shared history. She and Raasha held back the University's grave news
  until the Empress pressed them, then laid out the four missing students. Visibly
  angry, concerned, and nervous throughout — and asked to wait outside when the
  Empress cleared the room.
