# Seris

**Role:** Tiefling Griffin rider · **Affiliation:** the Griffin riders of
[the Jade District](../locations/the-jade-district.md)

## Overview

A tiefling Griffin rider, addressed with courtly deference as "M'lady," who rides
her griffon [Glidershade](glidershade.md). She
survived a brush with death in an earlier session — saved by
[Krum](../pcs/krum.md), while [Prance](../pcs/prance-galavant.md) so impressed her
Griffin that the beast itself took a liking to the party.

She is more than a rider: a former commander turned war mage and professor at
[the University](../locations/the-university.md), she once served as
[Behnsun Payne](../pcs/behnsun-payne.md)'s superior and helped rescue him and
[Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md) and bring them to the city years ago. She now sits on
the Empress's council alongside the arcane administrator
[Raasha Vaad](raasha-vaad.md).

## Session History

- **[The Tidewoven Amulet](../../sessions/2026-07-03.md)** — At Prance's request, she
  took to the air and scouted the city (rolling a natural 20 on perception),
  reporting that [the University](../locations/the-university.md) stood strong,
  [the Slums](../locations/the-slums.md) were worse off than usual, and
  [the Jade District](../locations/the-jade-district.md) — home of the Griffins
  and [the Water Temple](../locations/the-water-temple.md) — was under the
  heaviest attack. She then flew to defend the Jade District, sending the party
  toward the Slums.
- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — Sat the Empress's
  council with [Raasha Vaad](raasha-vaad.md), vouching for the party and helping
  bring the plight of the four missing students to light. Visibly angry, concerned,
  and nervous throughout.
