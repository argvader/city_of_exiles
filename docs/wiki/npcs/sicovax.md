# Sicovax

**Role:** Leader of [the Undying Flame](../factions/the-undying-flame.md)
· **Status:** Unseen — said to reside beyond
[the Redstone Narrows](../locations/redstone-narrows.md)

## Overview

The name behind the cult. Nobody in the party has seen Sicovax, and nothing is
known of what he looks like; everything here comes from one captured student
speaking under a *suggestion* spell, and from a fey voice in an amulet.

[Varrek Ashtongue](varrek-ashtongue.md) named him as the leader of the Undying
Flame and placed him somewhere past an entrance in the Redstone Narrows. Saying
even that much burned Varrek's mouth from the inside and brought up blood, which
suggests Sicovax does not leave his followers' silence to chance.

## Goals

**Revenge.** That was Varrek's single-word answer to what Sicovax wants with the
abducted water priestess [Naivara Tidewoven](naivara-tidewoven.md).

## Old So-and-So

The myth of the Undying Flame tells of two great powers who contested an elven
woman of the desert, and of the loser, whose true name was never known.
[The Amulet Speaker](the-amulet-speaker.md) — the winner, and Naivara's father —
has only ever been able to refer to that rival as "this old so-and-so." When the
name Sicovax was put to him, the Speaker supposed it was the same being: *"Me and
the woman we competed over, we sired Naivara. He's jealous that I won her heart."*

If that holds, the priestess was not taken for the city's water. She was taken to
hurt her father.
