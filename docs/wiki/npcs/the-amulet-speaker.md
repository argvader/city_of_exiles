# The Amulet Speaker

**Role:** Fey being trapped in the Tidewoven amulet · **Status:** Bound to
[Prance Galavant](../pcs/prance-galavant.md)

## Overview

A fey being imprisoned inside a gold, teardrop-shaped water amulet dropped by
[Naivara Tidewoven](naivara-tidewoven.md) as she was abducted. It speaks only to
whoever holds it to their ear — where one hears the sound of ocean waves — and it
is, by its own account, Naivara's father.

## Personality

Prickly, grief-stricken, and imperious. It rejected [Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md)
flatly ("you were not chosen") and instead chose Prance ("You will do. You must
save my daughter"). When Adarius called it names, it snapped back: "I have
feelings, and my daughter was just kidnapped. I'm having a bad day."

## The Old Rivalry

Shown the skeletal-hand-and-flame sigil painted in
[the Undercroft](../locations/the-undercroft.md), the Speaker knew it at once —
and for the first time named an enemy: *"I thought it might be him. This old
so-and-so, never could let it go. He's always been jealous of me and my lover. If
he's behind my daughter being taken, then what is going on in this city? We are
in more trouble than we thought."* The name itself came out muffled, unspeakable;
[Prance](../pcs/prance-galavant.md) has settled on calling him **old so-and-so**.

Set against [Adarius](../pcs/adarius.md)'s recollection of the myth of
[the Undying Flame](../factions/the-undying-flame.md), the Speaker is the being
who won the elven woman of the desert — and the cult now moving on the city
belongs to the one who lost.

## Relationships

- **Father of [Naivara Tidewoven](naivara-tidewoven.md)**, the abducted water
  priestess.
- Recognized by **[Frederick](frederick.md)** — Fizz's own fey amulet — as kin;
  the two spirits are "brothers," and Frederick confirmed that
  [Fizz](../pcs/fizz.md) has also been "chosen."

## Session History

- **[The Tidewoven Amulet](../../sessions/2026-07-03.md)** — Revealed by Adarius's
  *identify* as "something trapped inside." Chose Prance, who donned the amulet
  and swore an oath to rescue the priestess, feeling a spark of magic settle into
  place.
- **[The Undying Four](../../sessions/2026-09-02.md)** — Identified the cult's
  sigil as his old rival's, then turned on Prance for treating the rescue as a
  performance: *"This isn't some play, rabbit boy. This isn't some grand opera.
  This is my daughter."* When Prance answered that he would save her at the moment
  most advantageous to the story, the amulet's magic pulsed once and **weakened**.
  Its power, it warned, comes at the price of making the daughter the priority.
