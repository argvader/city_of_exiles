# Tirakar Sun

**Role:** Owner of the arena, council member of [Utsa Paradisa](../locations/utsa-paradisa.md)

## Overview

The proprietor of the city's fighting arena and a seat on the Empress's council.
Where the rest of the room worried over the abducted priestess, Tirakar fretted
about his own business — some of his caged monsters killed in the attack, others
escaped, and a number of prisoners loosed into the city along with them. The
Empress cut him off sharply for raising ledgers while Naivara was gone, but the
loose monsters and escaped prisoners he mentioned are now problems prowling the
streets.

## Session History

- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — Reported to the
  council that the assault had emptied part of his arena: dead beasts, escaped
  monsters, and freed prisoners now scattered through Utsa Paradisa.
