# Varrek Ashtongue

**Role:** Illusion student at [the University](../locations/the-university.md)
· **Status:** Missing — and close

## Overview

A brass dragonborn illusionist, one of the four students who disappeared from the
off-campus dorm. Of the four, he is the only one the party has evidence is still
in the city — and still watching them.

## The Glyph

While the party slept in the students' beds, someone laid a **glyph of warding**
on the dorm door. When they shut it behind them on the way out, a great **V**
flared in the circle and a wall of force blasted all five of them ten feet across
the campus grounds. [Krum](../pcs/krum.md), thinking on how deeply and how badly
the whole party had slept, realised the obvious: whoever set it had been near
enough to do it while they lay unconscious.
