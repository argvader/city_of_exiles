# Varrek Ashtongue

**Role:** Illusion student at [the University](../locations/the-university.md);
member of [the Undying Flame](../factions/the-undying-flame.md)
· **Status:** Captured — bound, gagged, and held by the party

## Overview

A brass dragonborn illusionist, one of the four students who disappeared from the
off-campus dorm, and the only one who stayed in the city to watch the party.
Classmates know him as a braggart: he talks endlessly about how good a spellcaster
he is and leaves a great **V** as a signature on everything he casts. He is vain
enough to be flattered by a compliment on his laugh from the man pinning his arms,
and it was vanity — a forged notice offering him a certificate — that brought him
to the library at all.

His kit is an illusionist's: *invisibility*, *misty step* (verbal only, which is
why he is gagged), the glyph of warding, and a brass dragonborn's fire breath and
resistance to flame.

## The Glyph

While the party slept in the students' beds, someone laid a **glyph of warding**
on the dorm door. When they shut it behind them on the way out, a great **V**
flared in the circle and a wall of force blasted all five of them ten feet across
the campus grounds. [Krum](../pcs/krum.md), thinking on how deeply and how badly
the whole party had slept, realised the obvious: whoever set it had been near
enough to do it while they lay unconscious. Asked about it later, Varrek was
unrepentant: *"What's wrong with home security?"*

## What He Said

Under [Fizz](../pcs/fizz.md)'s *suggestion* — and visibly against some other
compulsion, with fire burning in his mouth and blood on his teeth — Varrek gave
up the following:

- The four students never called themselves *the Undying Four*; that name is the
  party's own invention. They are simply members of the Undying Flame.
- [The Redstone Narrows](../locations/redstone-narrows.md) hold the entrance to
  the place where **[Sicovax](sicovax.md)** resides.
- Sicovax is the cult's leader, and what he wants with
  [Naivara Tidewoven](naivara-tidewoven.md) is **revenge**.
- The other three students, he imagines, have fled. He does not know where.

He also said, truthfully, that the party definitely should not kill him and that
he was probably not going to help them.
