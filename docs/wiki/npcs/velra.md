# Velra

**Role:** Drow confidante and guardian of [Empress Serenity](empress-serenity.md)

## Overview

A hooded drow woman who guards the Jade Pyramid's secret ways and never lets her
guard down. She met the party at a concealed door in the pyramid wall, opening it
with a blood-keyed ring, and led them through the pyramid's shifting, illusion-warped
halls — corridors that fold and mislead anyone who doesn't know the trick. In the
war room she trusts no one but the Empress, watching the whole council with a cool,
appraising eye.

She takes a peculiar interest in [Fizz](../pcs/fizz.md): she licked the blood he'd
tried to sneakily wipe from her ring-hand, later put a dagger to his throat with a
smiling "you should not trust anyone, my dear mushroom — but I think you may be
worth my time," and traded winks with him across the room. Whether that is
affection, appraisal, or a warning, not even Fizz can tell.
