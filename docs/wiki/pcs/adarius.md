# Adarius

**Player:** Josh · **Ancestry:** Human · **Class:** Wizard

The party's arcane mind — sharp, skeptical, and rarely without a dry remark.

## Overview

Adarius is the group's resident spellcaster and its loudest voice of caution.
When the party recovered a mysterious talking amulet, it was Adarius who
ritually cast *identify* on it — and Adarius who immediately wanted to sell the
"talking asshole necklace" rather than answer its call.

## Ties to the City

His parents live in [the Slums](../locations/the-slums.md) of
[Utsa Paradisa](../locations/utsa-paradisa.md) — a hardy father the party came to
know as "Bob" and a nimble mother. During the defense of the Slums, his father
was caught in a magma creature's fire and badly burned, but survived thanks to
the party and the healing magic of [the Dragonborn Band](../npcs/the-dragonborn-band.md).
Adarius is also said to have connections at [the University](../locations/the-university.md).

## Session History

- **[The Tidewoven Amulet](../../sessions/2026-07-03.md)** — Cast *identify* on the
  teardrop amulet dropped by [Naivara Tidewoven](../npcs/naivara-tidewoven.md),
  learning that "something is trapped inside" — a fey being that rejected him
  outright ("you were not chosen"). Spent the session urging the party to sell
  the amulet rather than take up its quest, and rolled a dirty nat 20 on Insight
  to remain thoroughly unconvinced.
- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — Revealed his Bladesinger
  training, dancing through the fight while humming *Pure Imagination*. Led the
  investigation of the missing students' dorm, endured [Gorvak Skybinder](../npcs/gorvak-skybinder.md)'s
  jabs about his old stable job, and was rewarded by [Empress Serenity](../npcs/empress-serenity.md)
  with a full University scholarship and a promise to rehouse his parents. The
  trapped room nearly killed him — smothered unconscious by an animated rug before
  [Fizz](fizz.md)'s healing word brought him back.
- **[The Undying Four](../../sessions/2026-09-02.md)** — The party's researcher in
  full: identified the party's new *potion of invisibility*, recalled from a
  history check the myth of [the Undying Flame](../factions/the-undying-flame.md)
  and what it means for [Naivara](../npcs/naivara-tidewoven.md)'s parentage, then
  ritually cast *comprehend languages* to pull **the Redstone Narrows** out of the
  cult's coded notes — the tar country where he was made to labour as a child.
  Wore the remains of the murderous rug out as a poncho. Capped the day by
  attempting to intimidate a message board with a natural 1, producing a notice so
  badly misspelled the party wondered aloud how he got into the school.
- **[Varrek's Very Bad Day](../../sessions/2026-10-13.md)** — Lost the coin-flip vote to
  go to the council first and was called a nerdy little wizard for it. In the
  library he tried to end the fight gently — "Lad, the intent isn't to kill you" —
  with a *charm person* that met a natural 20. In the street chase he put a *fire
  bolt* down an alley into [Varrek Ashtongue](../npcs/varrek-ashtongue.md), who
  knew him: *"Adarius, is that you, boy?"* Stood ready with a bottle of ink to
  mark the invisible man, then argued for the plan the party adopted — show the
  council the letter, keep the prisoner out of sight, and watch the room for a
  flicker of recognition. As the party's wizard, he has the job of guarding
  Varrek. *(Josh was away; Gary played Adarius for the session.)*
