# Fizz

**Player:** Kevin · **Ancestry:** Myconid · **Class:** Bard

A friendly mushroom-man performer who measures success by how many friends he
can make.

## Overview

Fizz supports the party with bardic inspiration, *song of rest*, and a light
crossbow, and carries a respectable medicine kit. He has a fanbase among the
common folk of [the Slums](../locations/the-slums.md) — "a lot of my customers,"
as he put it when the party chose where to make their stand.

## Frederick

Fizz carries **[Frederick](../npcs/frederick.md)**, a talking wooden amulet that
houses a fey being. Over the course of the session it was revealed that Frederick
has "chosen" Fizz — marking him, like [Prance](prance-galavant.md), as bound to a
fey vessel and its purposes.

## Session History

- **[The Tidewoven Amulet](../../sessions/2026-07-03.md)** — Freed
  [Major Jamieson](../npcs/major-jamieson.md)'s sewn-shut mouth with a delicate
  medicine check, felled a suture fly with a clean crossbow shot, and used
  Frederick to confirm that both he and Prance had been "chosen" by kindred fey
  spirits.
- **[The Vanished Scholars](../../sessions/2026-08-11.md)** — Leaned hard into his
  College of Eloquence gift: soothed the tense council with music, sweet-talked
  [Horace Steelwinter](../npcs/horace-steelwinter.md) into a gift of healing potions
  (persuasion 26), and began a wary flirtation with
  [Velra Nighthollow](../npcs/velra-nighthollow.md), who
  put a dagger to his throat and decided he "may be worth my time." His rapport
  spores even let him eavesdrop on the [Amulet Speaker](../npcs/the-amulet-speaker.md).
  Ended the dorm brawl by decomposing and eating the defeated books.
- **[The Undying Four](../../sessions/2026-09-02.md)** — The session's schemer.
  Identified the *potion of invisibility*, ate a jar of bat guano and learned from
  it that the students were hoarding fire components, and — rather than report the
  cult to a court he does not trust — devised the plan the campaign now turns on: a
  **forged letter in the missing students' own voices**, luring whoever answers to
  the Lucky Scorpion wearing a black bandana, signed *the Undying Four*. Read
  [Holgren](../npcs/holgren.md) as genuine, posted a fake certificate notice to bait
  out [Varrek Ashtongue](../npcs/varrek-ashtongue.md), and heard
  [Frederick](../npcs/frederick.md) ask him why the glory has to be Prance's.
  Admitted the honest answer: he is two years old, and books just beat him half to
  death.
- **[Varrek's Very Bad Day](../../sessions/2026-10-13.md)** — Carried the session and
  burned most of his spell slots doing it. Paid a student a gold piece to be
  searched, opened on [Varrek Ashtongue](../npcs/varrek-ashtongue.md) with a
  *vicious mockery* about student hygiene, and talked the dragonborn's fire
  breath back down with a persuasion of 22 at disadvantage. His *suggestion* is
  what finally produced the name [Sicovax](../npcs/sicovax.md) — and a mouthful of
  blood across his face. In the chase his first *sleep* fell short and his second,
  a 31, dropped Varrek in [Prance](prance-galavant.md)'s arms. Then he argued,
  three different ways, that the prisoner should be killed in the alley before a
  traitor on the council could free him, and offered to do it himself. He lost
  the argument and proposed [Velra Nighthollow](../npcs/velra-nighthollow.md) as
  the jailer instead. Wonders whether reading everyone's minds is making him
  cynical — or whether it is mother's magic.
